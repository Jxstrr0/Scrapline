## 0.1.47.0: the driving school (Kelbo, from the pitch)

A fifth page on the Fight tab, **School**: eight short drills, each scored bronze, silver or gold.

- **The drills** (`DRILLS` in `src/sim/school.js`): Round the pit (eight rings round an open pit), Stop on the mark (four marks that only count once you've all but stopped), Hold the line (stay in a ring while a brick shoves you, 20 s), On target (biggest single hit on a parked box, 15 s), Into the pit (push a parked box in), Back on your wheels (start upside down; self-right, reach a ring, get turned over again), Sideways (mecanum: a ring only counts while the nose points up the floor), Mind the grate (six crossings of a flame grate; a second on it lit costs three).
- **The bot:** every drill is driven in the school's own beetleweight (`schoolBot`: a test build, changed where the drill needs it), in your team's colours. The same bot for everyone, so a medal is driving and not parts. Decided without asking; see plan section 1.
- **In the sim:** a drill is a round in the real fight sim. `newFight({drill: id})` lays the drill's floor (walls, and a pit or a grate where it has one), puts the bots where it wants them, and may run with one bot. `stepFight` hands the end of each step to `drillStep`, which ticks off the steps (`go` rings with `face`, `slow` and `flip`; `hit`; `pit`) and ends the round with reason `"drill"`. Three kinds: `time` (the clock starts at your first touch of the controls; less is better), `hold` (seconds in the ring) and `hit` (joules). `F.drill` is the state the views draw from. The log carries `drill`, so a drill replays.
- **The instructor:** `drillAuto(F)` is a plain path follower that drives any drill. The node test runs it to prove every drill can be done; **Watch the instructor** plays its run back as a replay.
- **Scores** (the instructor's): 5.55 s, 4.87 s, 18.95 s of 20, 118 J, 3.35 s, 7.33 s, 5.52 s, 13.42 s. Gold is set a little slower than these, silver and bronze well slower. They are not tested with thumbs on a phone.
- **The page** (`src/app/school.js`): a row per drill showing its medal; open one for what to do, a tip, the three medal scores, your best and tries, **Drive it** and **Watch the instructor**. In a drill the clock counts up (time drills), the note under it says which ring you're on, and there is no announcer. The bell's banner gives the medal and what the next one needs.
- **The views:** rings on the floor on every tier (`drillRings` in `src/vis/arena.js`; the 2D view draws them too). The ring you're on is clay, the ones to come are faint, a hold ring is lilac. The camera keeps the next two rings in the frame.
- **Save:** `S.school` = `{<drill id>: {m, best, n}}` (best medal 0 to 3, best score, tries). `SAVE_V` 16; `MIGRATE[15]` adds it empty. A stopped drill and the instructor's run score nothing.
- **Tests:** `tools/sim-test.js` checks every drill's bots are legal, the medal tables are in order, the instructor medals twice the same, the log replays to the same score, and hands off the stick passes nothing.

## 0.1.50.0: a calmer fight camera (play-test, part two of four)

The play-tester's bot slid half off the left edge beside the pit while the view framed the opponent, and the view kept changing its zoom. The camera never turns (it always looks up the arena at one angle); what moved was the distance and the aim. Three small changes in `arenaTick` (`src/vis/arena.js`), tuned by one table, `CAM`:

- **The near side is narrower.** The distance was worked out from the width of the box round the bots at the aim point, but the side of the box nearer the camera is seen from closer, so it is narrower on the screen: a bot in a near corner could be cut off. The distance now allows for it (`CAM.near`).
- **A calmer zoom.** The distance the camera settles on follows the bots out at once, but only comes back in once they are 14% closer (`CAM.band`), and closes in more slowly (`CAM.inn`), so it no longer breathes in and out with every shove.
- **The bot you drive stays on the screen** (live fights and drills, `V.run.mark`; not replays): if the smoothed frame would cut it off at a side, or put it under the bot cards or the controls, the aim moves over by just that much, that frame (`CAM.edge` is the margin).
- Every tier, no cost. Replays and watched fights get the first two and frame both bots as before. No sim change, no save change.
